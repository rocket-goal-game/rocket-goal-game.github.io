Game Overview

Rocket Goal brings the adrenaline-pumping excitement of premium car soccer games (like Rocket League) directly to your browser. There is nothing to install and nothing to download. Whether you are at school, work, or home, you can jump straight into the arena and start scoring.

Featuring awesomely fun physics, high-quality visuals, and smooth performance, Rocket Goal challenges you to master aerial control, boost management, and strategic positioning to secure victory for your team.

Pro Tips for Victory

  1. Master the Boost: Your nitrous drains quickly! Don't hold the button down. collect the glowing gold coins scattered around the arena to refill your tank.
  2. Use Ball Cam: Toggle Spacebar to lock your camera onto the ball. This ensures you never lose sight of the play, even when driving back to defend.
  3. Defense Wins Games: You don't get extra points for being a "ball hog." If your teammate is attacking, stay back to defend against counter-attacks.
  4. Use the Walls: The arena walls are drivable! Use them to flank opponents or hit aerial shots from unexpected angles.
